Two international tracks. One continuous journey.

Al-Alson teaches American and British international programmes from kindergarten to Grade 12 — with Arabic, Islamic studies and the Holy Qur'an at the centre of the timetable throughout.

01 Stages

Every stage, on one campus journey.

Learning begins with wonder. Alongside early literacy and numeracy, French and computer learning start in the KG years — and an intensive KG3 programme prepares children thoroughly for Grade 1.

02 Curricula & accreditation

Choose the track. Keep the identity.

American track

A full American programme from KG to Grade 12, taught to international standards with continuous assessment and an American high-school diploma.

  • English language arts, mathematics, sciences, social studies
  • SAT preparation from Grade 7
  • Arabic & Islamic studies per Ministry requirements

British track

A complete British pathway culminating in IGCSE and A-Level examinations, taught by specialist subject teachers.

  • Cambridge & Pearson Edexcel examination routes
  • OxfordAQA options at senior level
  • University admissions guidance for the UK and worldwide

Egyptian languages track

For families seeking the Egyptian general curriculum in its languages version, offered at our Al-Murabba campus from KG to Grade 12.

  • Egyptian Ministry curriculum, languages stream
  • Full Arabic and Islamic studies programme
  • Same facilities, activities and care
Cognia accredited Cambridge AIAASC Saudi Ministry of Education — Category "A"

04 Assessment & support

Every student, properly seen.

Assessment & progress

Assessment serves learning: continuous classroom evaluation, formal examinations, and honest reporting to parents through weekly plans and scheduled meetings.

  • Weekly plans and continuous evaluation
  • Mock SAT testing from Grade 7, all year
  • Formal examinations by stage and track
  • Parent meetings and progress reporting

Guidance & wellbeing

A dedicated counseling and student-activity department operates in every section — supporting behaviour, wellbeing and our gifted students, with clinics in every building.

  • Student counseling unit in all sections
  • Dedicated gifted-students programme
  • Behaviour support plans with parents as partners
  • Medical clinics in every building, staffed all day
