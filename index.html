01 Who we are

For more than fifteen years, Al-Alson has prepared a generation that adapts to the changes of its society and the developments of the world — active and productive in its community.

Al-Alson International Schools is one of the distinguished international school groups in Riyadh, teaching across all stages — kindergarten through Grade 12 — for boys and girls across our Al-Olaya and Al-Murabba campuses. Since 2023 the schools have been part of Ataa Educational Company, one of the Kingdom's established educational groups.

Advanced academics sit alongside strong Arabic and Islamic identity: our students train on international curricula by day and compete in Qur'an and Sunnah competitions by choice — many earning advanced places at the Riyadh level.

02 Our approach

How we teach, and why.

Explore academics →
— 01

International curricula

American and British programmes taught from KG to Grade 12, accredited internationally and delivered by qualified, experienced teaching staff.

— 02

Faith & identity

Arabic, Islamic studies and the Holy Qur'an sit at the centre of the timetable — not at its edge. Values and etiquette are taught deliberately from the KG years.

— 03

Innovation & STEM

Smart interactive boards in classrooms, dedicated AI and robotics rooms, and STEM/STEAM methodology that teaches students to build, test and think for themselves.

— 04

Parents as partners

Positive, rapid communication with families, a student counseling unit in every section, and a dedicated programme for our gifted students.
