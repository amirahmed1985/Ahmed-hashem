Home/Admissions

A clear path from enquiry to first day.

We keep admissions simple, transparent and personal. Tell us about your child, visit us at Al-Olaya, and we will guide you through every step that follows.

01 How to apply

Five steps, no surprises.

1

Enquiry

Complete the enquiry form below or contact the school. We respond personally to every enquiry.

2

Campus visit

Tour the school, see lessons in progress and meet the people who would teach your child.

3

Application

Submit the application form together with the required documents — the checklist is below.

4

Assessment & interview

An age-appropriate assessment and a short interview, so we can know your child properly.

5

Offer & registration

Places are offered in writing. On acceptance, we handle the details of a smooth first day.

02 What you'll need

The document checklist.

Required documents

Applications are completed with the following, in line with Ministry of Education requirements:

  • Student's birth certificate
  • Complete vaccination record
  • Copies of both parents' identification
  • Student's passport or residence document
  • Four recent photographs (4×6)
  • Previous school records, where applicable

Age & entry criteria

Placement follows Ministry of Education age rules, by September of the entry year:

  • KG1: three years of age · KG2: four · KG3: five
  • Grade 1: two completed KG years, or success in the school's assessment
  • Transfers from accredited international schools: direct entry
  • Other transfers: placement test and interview

Note Exact requirements can vary by grade and track — the admissions office confirms your personal checklist when you enquire.
