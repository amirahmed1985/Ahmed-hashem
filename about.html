Home/About Us

A school with a clear sense of purpose.

Since 2009, Al-Alson International Schools have prepared generations of students in the heart of Riyadh — combining advanced international curricula with an unshaken Arabic and Islamic identity.

01 Our story

Established 2009. Growing with intent ever since.

Al-Alson International Schools was established in 2009 in Riyadh as an international school applying international curricula alongside Arabic and Islamic education. The name itself — الألسن, "the tongues" — speaks to the school's founding focus on language and communication as the doorway to every other discipline.

Growth has been deliberate. Today the schools serve around 2,700 students across 110 classrooms on roughly 20,000 m² of campus, across two locations in the capital: Al-Olaya, on Makkah Al-Mukarramah Road, and Al-Murabba — all stages, boys and girls, from kindergarten to Grade 12.

In 2023 the schools joined Ataa Educational Company, a Saudi educational group, opening a new chapter of investment in facilities, staff development and academic quality.

02 Vision & mission

What we are here to do.

Our vision

To prepare a generation with the minds, bodies and character to adapt to the changes of its society and the developments of the world — active, productive and responsible in its community.

Our mission

To provide high-quality international education with an Islamic perspective — meeting students' mental, spiritual and physical needs within a safe, caring and disciplined environment.

Our beliefs

Respect, responsibility and good relationships between students are rights for every child. Innovative teaching, teamwork with parents, and a safe environment that rewards good attendance and genuine achievement.
